Help & Support

Answers to common questions. Can't find yours? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. Ad-free play is restored on a new device or after reinstalling, as long as you use the same Apple ID or Google account. Coin packs are consumable and cannot be restored.

I bought coins but didn't receive them.

Make sure you are online and reopen the game; a purchase that was interrupted is delivered automatically on the next launch. If the coins still don't appear, contact us with the date of the purchase and your order or receipt number.

What does ad-free play remove?

It removes the ads shown between levels. Reward videos stay available, but only when you choose to watch one for coins or a hint.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new device?

Your levels, coins, friends and decorations are saved on your device. The game has no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app or changing devices starts a new journey. Ad-free play can be restored with Restore purchases.

Can I play offline?

Yes. All levels can be played without an internet connection. Ads, reward videos and purchases need a connection.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress. This erases levels, coins (including purchased coins), items and friend care. Ad-free play is kept. This cannot be undone.
